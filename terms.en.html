Aip Teleprompter Terms of Use and Subscriptions

Updated October 16, 2026

This app is provided by jessel and licensed under the Apple Standard End User License Agreement. The following explains app features, PRO purchases, and your responsibilities for content. Where this page conflicts with applicable Apple purchase or refund rules, those Apple rules apply.

Free and PRO access

In the free version, each visit to the teleprompter board or video prompting page allows a cumulative 6 minutes of playback. Pauses and countdowns do not count. PRO unlocks unlimited prompting, floating prompting across apps, local network hosting, and 4K recording when supported by the device and camera. A local network display or remote does not need a separate PRO purchase on the second device. Existing recordings, including previously recorded 4K files, can still be played and exported.

Purchases and management

PRO offers monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. All three unlock the same benefits. The actual price, currency, and billing period are shown in the localized Apple purchase information and system confirmation sheet. Subscriptions renew under Apple's rules until canceled in your Apple account subscription settings; uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. You can restore purchases in the app. Buying lifetime PRO does not automatically cancel an existing subscription. Check your subscription status yourself. Apple handles refunds and payment disputes; a refund or revocation may end PRO access.

Your content and third-party services

You retain rights in your scripts and recordings and are responsible for having permission to import, record, share, or send content to an AI provider. AI features use your own provider account and API key. The provider controls availability, charges, output, and policies and may charge you separately. Connect to trusted devices only on the local network. Follow applicable law and obtain necessary consent before recording or publishing content.
