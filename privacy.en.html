Aip Teleprompter Privacy Policy

Updated October 29, 2026

This policy explains how Aip Teleprompter, provided by jessel, handles information on iPhone and iPad. The app does not require an account. The developer currently does not operate cloud storage for scripts or recordings and has not integrated ads, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

On-device content and permissions

Scripts, folders, prompting settings, and videos recorded in the app are stored on your device. Camera and microphone access is used for preview, recording, and voice tracking. You can revoke access in iOS Settings. Voice tracking needs on-device speech recognition to be available. Floating prompting shows the text and controls you select in system-supported Picture in Picture or Live Activity surfaces.

AI services

When you configure an AI provider or check its models, the app sends your API key for authentication and a model query to that provider; no script is sent for this check. Before your first AI generation request, the app identifies the provider, its fixed endpoint domain, and the data to be sent, and asks for your explicit consent. After you agree, each generation you initiate sends the complete current script, your prompt, selected model, and API key directly to that provider. The full script is included even if your prompt does not refer to it. Requests also contain routine network and request metadata needed to operate the service. You can cancel, and you can revoke permission for future requests from the AI Magic screen. API keys are stored in the device Keychain; model and connection status are stored on the device. The developer does not receive these AI requests and does not use scripts for advertising or model training.

We integrate only providers whose published API terms offer privacy protections comparable to this policy: by default, these services do not use API prompts and responses to train models and limit routine retention of request content to what is needed for security and service operation. The app currently offers fixed endpoints for the OpenAI API, Anthropic Claude API, and Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Bailian / DashScope). Under the providers’ current published API rules, these services do not use API prompts and responses to train their foundation models by default. They may retain request content for a limited period for security, abuse monitoring, or service operation; default periods and exceptions differ. OpenAI or Anthropic account settings or feedback you submit may change how data is used; legal requirements, security, and enforcement of service policies may also require additional retention. Alibaba Cloud Model Studio’s default retention period for prompts and responses is no longer than 30 days, except where required by law or reasonably necessary for security. Before using a provider, review its OpenAI API data controls, Anthropic API training notice, Anthropic API retention notice, Alibaba Cloud Model Studio data-use FAQ, and Alibaba Cloud Model Studio retention policy. Providers may update their rules; revoking permission on your device does not affect data already received by a provider. Deleting a local script does not delete data already sent. To request deletion or stop future requests, manage your provider account or API key under that provider’s policies.

Local network and purchases

When you choose a local network host, display, or remote role, nearby devices may see your device name. Once connected, the app can send the selected script, display settings, playback progress, or remote commands to connected devices. The system handles the connection as an encrypted session; connect only to devices you trust. Apple processes PRO purchases, renewals, and refunds. The app uses StoreKit to read products and verified purchase access and does not receive your full payment card details.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

Selected recordings or content are sent to a recipient only when you use system sharing or export. On-device scripts and recordings remain until you delete them in the app or remove the app. iOS settings and system behavior govern backups and Keychain retention; uninstalling the app may not remove a saved API key. Revoke unused keys with the provider. Deleting a local script does not erase requests already sent to a provider; the developer cannot delete provider-held data for you, so use that provider’s deletion process. Apple-held data is governed by Apple’s policies.
