JesseTeleprompter Privacy Policy

Updated October 8, 2026

This policy explains how JesseTeleprompter, provided by jessel, handles information on iPhone and iPad. The app does not require an account. The developer currently does not operate cloud storage for scripts or recordings and has not integrated ads, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

On-device content and permissions

Scripts, folders, prompting settings, and videos recorded in the app are stored on your device. Camera and microphone access is used for preview, recording, and voice tracking. You can revoke access in iOS Settings. Voice tracking needs on-device speech recognition to be available. Floating prompting shows the text and controls you select in system-supported Picture in Picture or Live Activity surfaces.

AI services

Only when you configure and use an AI feature does the app send your selected prompt, related script content, chosen model, and API key directly to your selected AI provider or custom endpoint. API keys are stored in the device Keychain; provider URLs, models, and test status are stored on the device. A provider may process request content, keys, and network information under its own policy. Review that policy before use, and do not send content you do not wish to share with the provider. The developer does not receive these requests.

Local network and purchases

When you choose a local network host, display, or remote role, nearby devices may see your device name. Once connected, the app can send the selected script, display settings, playback progress, or remote commands to connected devices. The system handles the connection as an encrypted session; connect only to devices you trust. Apple processes PRO purchases, renewals, and refunds. The app uses StoreKit to read products and verified purchase access and does not receive your full payment card details.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

Selected recordings or content are sent to a recipient only when you use system sharing or export. On-device scripts and recordings remain until you delete them in the app or remove the app. iOS settings and system behavior govern backups and Keychain retention; uninstalling the app may not remove a saved API key. Revoke unused keys with the provider. For data held by a provider or Apple, follow their deletion policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date if app features or data handling change. For privacy questions, contact 646202121@qq.com.
