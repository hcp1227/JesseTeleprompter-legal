Aip Teleprompter Privacy Policy

Updated November 8, 2026

This policy explains how Aip Teleprompter, provided by jessel, handles information on iPhone and iPad. The app does not require an account. The developer currently does not operate cloud storage for scripts or recordings and has not integrated ads, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

On-device content and permissions

Scripts, folders, prompting settings, and videos recorded in the app are stored on your device. Camera and microphone access is used for preview, recording, and voice tracking. You can revoke access in iOS Settings. Voice tracking needs on-device speech recognition to be available. Floating prompting shows the text and controls you select in system-supported Picture in Picture or Live Activity surfaces.

Optional AI services: data, recipients, and purposes

AI is optional. Without your permission, the app does not send AI requests. Model checking and saving a provider connection send your API key for authentication and a model-list query to that provider to verify access; they do not send a script. Generating or revising a script sends the complete current script, your selected or entered prompt, selected model, and API key directly to that provider to produce a result preview. The complete script is included even if the prompt does not mention it. Personal information you include in a script or prompt is also sent. The app does not attach your recordings, photos, contacts, or microphone audio to AI requests.

These HTTPS requests also expose routine connection and request information to the provider, such as your public IP address, request time, and API/client headers. A provider may associate requests with the account identified by your API key for authentication, billing, security, and abuse prevention. The developer does not receive or proxy AI requests, sell this content, or use it for advertising or model training. Keys are stored in the device Keychain; models, connection status, and consent records are stored on the device. Generated text remains a preview until you choose to apply or copy it.

Permission and your choices

Before connecting to a provider and before generating content, an in-app confirmation identifies the recipient and fixed endpoint, the data, and the purpose, with links to that provider’s data policy and service terms and this policy. Canceling sends no request for that action. Permission covers subsequent requests you initiate for the same disclosed service; it does not start automatic generation. Material changes to the disclosed data, recipient, purposes, or provider-policy information require renewed permission. You can revoke connection permission in AI provider settings and content-sharing permission in AI Magic. Revoking prevents future requests until you agree again; it does not erase data already sent. API keys and model choices are preserved when permission is revoked.

Providers and safeguards

The app uses only the following fixed API services. We require recipients to provide the same or equal protection for shared personal data as described here: processing for the disclosed service and necessary security or legal purposes, confidentiality and appropriate security measures, restricted access, and retention and deletion controls. We assess their published API agreements and data policies for these protections. This is not a promise of zero retention or a separate negotiated agreement. Provider account settings, optional data-sharing programs, and legal or security exceptions can affect processing; review the applicable terms before sending sensitive content.

  • OpenAI API — api.openai.com/v1. API content is not used for training by default. Standard abuse-monitoring logs may contain content and are normally retained for up to 30 days, with exceptions. Account opt-ins and model-specific controls can differ. See API data controls and the services agreement.
  • Anthropic Claude API — api.anthropic.com. Commercial API terms prohibit training on customer content. Standard API inputs and outputs are deleted within 30 days, with contractual, legal, and safety exceptions; flagged violations may be held longer. Voluntary feedback is subject to separate rules. See the API training notice, retention notice, and commercial terms.
  • Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Bailian / DashScope) — dashscope.aliyuncs.com/compatible-mode/v1, the China (Beijing) endpoint. The China-site service agreement is with 通义云启（杭州）信息技术有限公司. Its business-data terms limit processing to service instructions and legal requirements, prohibit unauthorized training use, and provide security and deletion protections. Call data may be stored as required by law; these China-site terms do not promise a universal 30-day limit. Separate Token Plan / Coding Plan programs have different terms. See the China-site privacy notice and service agreement.

Providers may process data outside your country or region under their agreements and infrastructure arrangements. Their availability and account requirements vary. The app does not enroll you in optional training or feedback programs. To request deletion of provider-held data, use that provider’s account controls or support process; deleting a local script or revoking app permission cannot delete provider-held copies. You may stop using AI without losing local editing or prompting features.

Local network and purchases

When you choose a local network host, display, or remote role, nearby devices may see your device name. Once connected, the app can send the selected script, display settings, playback progress, or remote commands to connected devices. The system handles the connection as an encrypted session; connect only to devices you trust. Apple processes PRO purchases, renewals, and refunds. The app uses StoreKit to read products and verified purchase access and does not receive your full payment card details.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

Selected recordings or content are sent to a recipient only when you use system sharing or export. On-device scripts and recordings remain until you delete them in the app or remove the app. iOS settings and system behavior govern backups and Keychain retention; uninstalling the app may not remove a saved API key. Revoke unused keys with the provider. Deleting a local script does not erase requests already sent to a provider; the developer cannot delete provider-held data for you, so use that provider’s deletion process. Apple-held data is governed by Apple’s policies.
