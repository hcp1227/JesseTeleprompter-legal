Aip Teleprompter Support and Contact

For help with the app, purchases, or feature feedback, email 646202121@qq.com. Include your device model, system and app versions, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send API keys, passwords, or complete private scripts.

Purchases and restoration

Tap Restore Purchases on the PRO page and check that your device uses the Apple Account used for the purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple's subscription settings.

Local network connection

Connect the host and display devices to the same local network, and allow Local Network access when prompted. After a connection loss, return to the app and keep both devices awake while they reconnect.

AI services

AI features require an API key for the provider you choose. If a connection fails, check the key and provider account status. Never email your key.
